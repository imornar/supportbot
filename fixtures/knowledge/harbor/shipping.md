# Shipping

Harbor Goods ships physical orders from the Portland warehouse. These rules are the ones support should quote.

## Cutoff and dispatch days

Orders placed on a weekday before 14:00 Pacific Time leave the warehouse the same day. Orders placed after 14:00, or on Saturday or Sunday, leave on the next weekday. The warehouse is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. An order placed on the business day before a closure waits until the warehouse reopens.

Dispatch means the carrier has the parcel. It does not mean the parcel has arrived.

## Methods

Standard shipping is 3 to 5 business days after dispatch inside the contiguous United States. Expedited shipping is 2 business days after dispatch. Overnight shipping, where the checkout offered it, is the next business day after dispatch. Business days exclude weekends and the closures listed above.

Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories use standard shipping only. Those deliveries take 5 to 10 business days after dispatch. Harbor Goods does not ship outside the United States.

## Tracking

A tracking number is sent to the email on the order after the carrier scans the parcel. That email can take until the evening of the dispatch day. If a customer says the shop showed "label created" but the carrier still has no scan, ask them to wait until the next weekday before opening a trace.

## Address changes

Support can change the ship-to address only before the parcel is dispatched. After dispatch, the customer has to use the carrier's intercept options. Do not promise that an intercept will succeed.

## Split shipments

A single order can leave in more than one parcel when an item is restocked later. Each parcel has its own tracking number. The customer is not charged a second shipping fee for the restocked parcel.
