# Damaged or missing delivery

Use this article when the parcel arrived damaged, the contents do not match the order, or the carrier marked the parcel delivered and the customer does not have it. A change of mind is a return, not a damage claim.

## Damaged on arrival

If the customer reports damage within 7 days of the delivery scan, Harbor Goods replaces the item or refunds it. The customer needs the order number and two photos: the item, and the outside of the parcel showing the label. Support does not need the customer to send the damaged item back unless a specialist asks for it.

Open a replacement when the same item is in stock. Open a refund when it is not. Do not ask the customer to file the carrier claim themselves. Harbor files that claim.

## Wrong item

A wrong item is Harbor's error. The customer may keep or discard the wrong item if support says so in the ticket. Ship the correct item with expedited shipping at no charge. If the customer prefers a refund, refund the item and the original outbound shipping fee.

## Missing after a delivered scan

Wait one business day after the carrier's delivered scan before opening a trace. Packages are sometimes scanned at the local depot and delivered the next morning. After that day, open a trace with the tracking number. If the carrier confirms a loss, replace or refund the order as a damage claim. If the carrier confirms delivery to the address on the order, tell the customer Harbor cannot ship a second parcel until the trace is closed.

## Orders that never got a tracking number

If the order is still "processing" after two business days past the shipping cutoff, check whether an item is waiting on restock. Tell the customer the restock date shown on the order. If there is no restock date and no tracking number, escalate the order to the warehouse queue. Do not promise a ship date that the order page does not show.
