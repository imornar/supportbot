# Returns

Harbor Goods accepts returns of unused items in the original packaging. These are the rules support should follow. Do not invent an exception for a channel or a promotion that this article does not name.

## Window

The customer has 30 days from the delivery date shown by the carrier. A return request opened on day 30 is still in the window. Day 31 is too late for a refund. The delivery date is the carrier's delivery scan, not the day the customer says they opened the box.

## How to start a return

The customer starts the return from the order page in their account, or by writing to support with the order number. Support creates the return authorization and emails a prepaid label for addresses in the contiguous United States. Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories can still return the item, but the label is not prepaid. The customer pays postage and can deduct the actual postage receipt from the refund, up to the original standard shipping amount.

## Condition

Items must be unused, unwashed, and in the original packaging. Beauty and food items cannot be returned once the seal is open, even inside the 30 day window. A customer who received the wrong item, or a defective item, is covered by the damaged delivery article instead of this one.

## Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds show within 5 to 10 business days after the warehouse receives the parcel. Store credit, when the customer chose it at checkout, returns as store credit on the same account. Harbor Goods does not refund the original outbound shipping fee unless the return reason is Harbor's error.

## Exchanges

An exchange is a return plus a new order. The new order ships after the warehouse receives the original item, unless the customer pays for the replacement up front. Do not ship a free replacement before the original item is scanned at the warehouse.
